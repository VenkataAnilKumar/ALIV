COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

Document Type: Lease Agreement
Vendor / Landlord: Acme Commercial Properties LLC
Client / Tenant: Redstone Enterprises Inc.
Effective Date: 2025-03-01
Expiry Date: 2026-03-01

--- TERMS ---

1. PREMISES
   Acme Commercial Properties LLC ("Landlord") hereby leases to Redstone Enterprises Inc.
   ("Tenant") the premises located at 400 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 12, San Francisco, CA 94105.

2. TERM
   The lease term commences on March 1, 2025 and expires on March 1, 2026.
   Tenant must provide written notice of renewal no later than 60 days prior to expiry.

3. RENEWAL
   Tenant has one option to renew for an additional 12-month term at the then-current market rate,
   subject to Landlord approval. Renewal notice must be delivered in writing by January 1, 2026.

4. RENT
   Monthly base rent: $8,400 USD, payable on the first of each month.
   Late payment fee: 1.5% of outstanding balance per month.

5. PENALTY CLAUSE
   Failure to vacate the premises within 5 business days after lease expiry shall result in a
   holdover penalty of $2,800 per day until the premises are fully vacated and keys returned.

6. TERMINATION
   Either party may terminate with 60 days written notice. Early termination by Tenant triggers
   a fee equal to 2 months' base rent ($16,800).

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
   The financial terms of this agreement are confidential and shall not be disclosed to any
   third party without written consent of both parties.
