SUPPLY AGREEMENT

Document Type: Supply Agreement
Supplier: Brightline Components GmbH
Customer: Redstone Enterprises Inc.
Effective Date: 2024-01-20
Expiry Date: 2027-01-20
Contract Value: EUR 412,500

1. SUPPLY
   Supplier shall manufacture and deliver the components listed in Schedule A at the prices
   stated therein, subject to annual indexation.

2. TERM AND RENEWAL
   This Agreement expires on 20 January 2027. Customer must give notice of renewal no later
   than 20 December 2026; otherwise the Agreement ends on its expiry date.

3. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
   For each week of delay beyond the confirmed delivery date, Supplier shall pay liquidated
   damages of 0.5% of the value of the delayed delivery, up to a maximum of 5%.

4. TERMINATION
   Either party may terminate this Agreement upon 60 days written notice in the event of a
   material breach that remains uncured.

5. GOVERNING LAW
   This Agreement is governed by the laws of Germany.
