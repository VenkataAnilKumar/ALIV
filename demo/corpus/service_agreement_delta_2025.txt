Document Type: Service Agreement
Vendor: Delta Advisory Group
Client / Counterparty: Pinnacle Fund Management LLC
Effective Date: 2025-01-10

--- TERMS ---

1. SERVICES
   Delta Advisory Group ("Advisor") agrees to provide fund performance analytics,
   regulatory compliance reporting, and investor relations support to Pinnacle Fund
   Management LLC ("Client") throughout the term of this Agreement.

2. TERM
   This Agreement commences January 10, 2025 and expires December 31, 2025.
   Either party must provide 90 days written notice to renew for a subsequent term.

3. FEES
   Monthly retainer: $22,000 USD. Billed on the 1st of each month.
   Ad hoc advisory work billed at $450/hour, pre-approved in writing by Client.

4. PENALTY CLAUSE
   A penalty of $5,000 per day shall apply for delivery delays on quarterly compliance
   reports exceeding five business days beyond the agreed submission deadline.
   Cumulative penalties shall not exceed $60,000 per calendar quarter.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
   All deliverables produced under this Agreement are work-for-hire and become the
   exclusive property of Pinnacle Fund Management LLC upon full payment.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
   Advisor's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid in the
   three months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
   Disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration in the State of Delaware under
   the rules of the American Arbitration Association.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY
   All fund performance data, investor information, and portfolio strategies shared
   under this Agreement are strictly confidential and subject to the separate NDA
   executed between the parties on December 15, 2024.
