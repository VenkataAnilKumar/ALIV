MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of December 15, 2025
between Northwind Logistics Inc. ("Provider") and Redstone Enterprises Inc. ("Client").

1. SERVICES
   Provider shall deliver freight forwarding, customs brokerage and warehousing services
   described in each Statement of Work issued under this Agreement.

2. TERM
   This Agreement commences on December 15, 2025 and expires on December 15, 2026,
   unless renewed in writing by both parties.

3. RENEWAL
   Client may renew for one additional year by giving written notice no later than
   November 15, 2026.

4. FEES
   Annual contract value: $186,000 USD, invoiced monthly in arrears.
   Late payments accrue interest at 1% per month on the outstanding balance.

5. SERVICE LEVELS AND PENALTIES
   If on-time delivery falls below 97% in any calendar month, Provider shall pay a penalty
   equal to 5% of that month's fees, capped at $9,300 per month.

6. TERMINATION
   Either party may terminate for convenience on ninety (90) days' written notice.

7. GOVERNING LAW
   This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Washington, without regard
   to its conflict of laws principles.
