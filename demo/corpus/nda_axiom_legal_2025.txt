--- TERMS ---

1. PURPOSE
   This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between
   Axiom Legal LLP ("Disclosing Party") and Meridian Capital Partners ("Receiving Party")
   for the purpose of evaluating a potential business engagement related to
   financial due diligence services.

2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
   "Confidential Information" includes all non-public financial data, client lists,
   deal structures, strategic plans, proprietary methodologies, and legal analyses
   shared between the parties during the evaluation period.

3. OBLIGATIONS
   The Receiving Party agrees to:
   a) Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence.
   b) Not disclose any Confidential Information to third parties without prior written consent.
   c) Use Confidential Information solely for the stated purpose of this Agreement.

4. TERM
   This Agreement is effective from July 15, 2025 and shall remain in effect until July 15, 2026,
   unless terminated earlier by mutual written agreement.

5. PENALTY CLAUSE
   Unauthorized disclosure of Confidential Information shall entitle the Disclosing Party to
   seek injunctive relief and liquidated damages of $50,000 per breach, in addition to any
   actual damages proven in a court of competent jurisdiction.

6. GOVERNING LAW
   This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.

7. RETURN OF INFORMATION
   Upon termination, all Confidential Information and copies must be returned or destroyed
   within 10 business days. Receiving Party must certify destruction in writing.
